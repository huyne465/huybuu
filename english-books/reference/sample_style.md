# ROXY ENGLISH – style reference (excerpt of the original 15-part sample unit "MY SCHOOL", Grade 2)

BIG QUESTION: What do you like about your school? / Em thích điều gì ở trường của mình?
Think and talk: What is your school's name? Is your school big or small? Who is your teacher?

VOCABULARY (English | Phonetic | Nghĩa tiếng Việt):
school | /skuːl/ | trường học ; classroom | /ˈklɑːsruːm/ | lớp học ; library | /ˈlaɪbreri/ | thư viện

SENTENCE PATTERNS:
Pattern 1 – This is my school. (Đây là trường của em.) Example: This is my school. It is beautiful.
Pattern 3 – There is a/an + noun. Examples: There is a library. There is a playground.

GRAMMAR IN USE: A. This is… We use "This is" to introduce one person, place, or thing.
✅ This is my school.  ❌ This are my school.

READING 1 – EASY: "MY SCHOOL"
My name is Ben. I am seven years old. I go to Green Primary School. My school is big and beautiful.
There are many classrooms in my school. There is a big playground, too. I have many friends at school.
My teacher is Ms. Anna. She is kind. I love my school very much.
(+ 10 vocabulary rows, + 10 questions)

SPEAK UP: 15 guiding questions + Speaking Model paragraph + "Talk about your school for 30–60 seconds."

REAL-LIFE CONVERSATIONS: Anna: Hi, Tom! Is this your school? / Tom: Yes, it is. / Anna: Wow! Your school is beautiful.

LISTEN & RESPOND: teacher's listening script (≈120 words) + 10 questions.

WRITE ABOUT YOU: "My School Profile" fill-in fields + writing frame of 10 sentence starters.

LIFE SKILL & VALUE: LOVE AND RESPECT YOUR SCHOOL – "At school, I can:" Listen to my teachers. Be kind to my friends…
ROXY VALUE QUESTION: What can you do to make your school a happier place?

ROXY PROJECT: MY DREAM SCHOOL POSTER – Step 1 Draw, Step 2 Write 5–8 sentences, Step 3 Present.

REVIEW: A. Complete the words (sch__l) B. Choose the correct answer (a/b/c) C. Put the words in order D. Answer about you.

I CAN…: □ I can say school words in English. □ I can use There is. …

UNIT MESSAGE: "SCHOOL IS A PLACE TO LEARN, TO LOVE, AND TO GROW." / Trường học là nơi em học hỏi, yêu thương và trưởng thành.
ROXY VALUE: Learn English – Learn to Love – Learn to Grow.
